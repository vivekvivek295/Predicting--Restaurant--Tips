# Predicting Restaurant Tips – Excel Project
## 1. Introduction

The Predicting Restaurant Tips project is an Excel-based data analysis project designed to understand the factors that influence restaurant tips. The dataset contains information such as gender, smoking status, day, time, party size, total bill, and tip amount. Excel PivotTables, charts, and regression analysis are used to analyze relationships within the data.

2. Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

To analyze restaurant tips based on different customer characteristics.
To compare total tips received from male and female customers.
To analyze tips according to meal time.
To study the relationship between total bill and tip amount.
To visualize the results using charts.
To use regression analysis for predicting restaurant tips.
3. Tools Used
Microsoft Excel
PivotTables
PivotCharts
Scatter Plot
Trendline
Regression Analysis
4. Data Analysis
Gender-wise Tip Analysis

The PivotTable shows that the total tips received from female customers were 244.51, while male customers contributed 485.07 in total tips.

The total tip amount was 729.58. Male customers therefore contributed approximately 66% of the total tips, while female customers contributed approximately 34%.

Time-wise Tip Analysis

The analysis also compares tips during dinner and lunch.

Dinner: 546.07 total tips
Lunch: 183.51 total tips

The results indicate that dinner generated substantially more total tips than lunch.

The gender and time analysis further shows:

Female dinner tips: 156.11
Male dinner tips: 389.96
Female lunch tips: 88.40
Male lunch tips: 95.11

This shows that dinner contributes the largest share of tips, particularly from male customers.

5. Tip and Total Bill Relationship

A scatter chart was created to study the relationship between total bill and tip.

The chart shows an overall positive relationship between the two variables. As the total bill increases, the tip amount generally tends to increase as well. The trendline supports this relationship, although individual customers may give different tip amounts for similar bills.

This relationship is useful for predicting the approximate tip amount from a customer's total bill.

6. Visualization

The project dashboard contains different visualizations, including:

Doughnut Chart: Displays the percentage contribution of tips by gender.
Scatter Plot: Shows the relationship between total bill and tip.
Column Chart: Compares tips based on gender and meal time.
PivotTables: Provide summarized numerical results.

These visualizations make the analysis easier to understand and help identify important patterns in the restaurant data.

7. Key Findings

The major findings from the analysis are:

Male customers contributed more total tips than female customers.
Dinner generated significantly higher total tips than lunch.
The total bill and tip amount have a positive relationship.
Higher restaurant bills generally result in higher tips.
PivotTables and charts provide an effective way to summarize and communicate the data.
8. Conclusion

The Predicting Restaurant Tips project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used for practical data analysis and prediction. PivotTables were used to summarize the data, while charts helped visualize important patterns and relationships.

The analysis shows that total bill, gender, and meal time provide useful information for understanding restaurant tipping behavior. The positive relationship between total bill and tip also indicates that regression analysis can be useful for predicting tips.

Overall, this project provides practical experience in data cleaning, PivotTable analysis, data visualization, correlation, and regression-based prediction using Excel.
